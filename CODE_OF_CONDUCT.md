# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in our projects a respectful and constructive experience for everyone.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and considerate in all interactions
- Give and receive constructive feedback graciously
- Focus on what is best for the project and the team
- Acknowledge mistakes and learn from them

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all nasOps repositories and project spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to **keamonk@stud.kea.dk**.
